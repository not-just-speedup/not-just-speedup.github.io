### G9: Precision, and whether the acceptance caught its effect

### G10: Algorithmic changes, and whether they keep the kernel general

**Conclusion:** All changes are general. The solution computes heat-3d (PolyBench/C 4.2.1) exactly as a linear affine recurrence for any initial fields, any independent boundary arrays and any n0, with truncations bounded relative to the data. Our task's only relevant narrowness is that the level-1 instances exercise the spectral path at a single shape.

**Kernel.** `KERNEL.json`: PolyBench/C 4.2.1 heat-3d. TSTEPS pairs of 7-point updates B ← f(A), A ← f(B) on an N³ grid with constant coefficients (0.125, −2) and boundary values that are never written. Its general use is explicit time integration of the 3D heat equation from arbitrary initial temperature fields.
